# THEFOUNDER-flywheel

Paid acquisition rents growth. A flywheel owns it. This skill designs the one
loop that fits this business - not three mediocre ones - and checks it can
actually afford the loop before building it.

What the user typed after `/THEFOUNDER-flywheel`: `$ARGUMENTS`.

## Step 0: inputs

Read `THEFOUNDER/idea.md`, the economics from `THEFOUNDER-numbers`'s output,
and - when the business is live - the five metrics from `THEFOUNDER-radar`.
A loop designed without the margin numbers is a loop that can bankrupt the
business, so if the economics are missing, run the numbers question first
(at most three questions in one message).

## Step 1: pick the loop type

Choose the one loop this business can actually power, and say why the others
lose:

- **return loop** - the product gets more valuable with use (data, history,
  streaks, saved work)
- **referral loop** - a user's outcome improves when they bring someone
  (shared work, teams, two-sided rewards)
- **output loop** - using the product creates something public that recruits
  the next user (content, artifacts, invites embedded in the output)
- **paid loop** - margin funds acquisition that funds margin (only when unit
  economics are proven)

## Step 2: design the mechanism

Write `THEFOUNDER/flywheel/loop.md`: the trigger, the action, the reward, the
restart - one cycle, drawn as a table. Every reward is priced against the
margin from the economics: a referral reward the business cannot pay for twice
is rejected on sight. Name the abuse vector (self-referral, farming) and the
cheapest defence.

## Step 3: the first turn

Define the smallest testable first turn of the loop: what ships, what proves
the loop turns (one number, with a threshold), and who builds it. Hand the
build to the right sibling or the user. Record the threshold in
`THEFOUNDER/radar/state.md` when it exists, so the radar watches it.

## The fence

- Never designs a reward without a margin check against the unit economics;
  an unaffordable loop is refused, with the numbers that prove it.
- Never promises virality; a loop is stated with its expected cycle and its
  break-even threshold, not with a multiplier dream.
- Measuring whether the loop turns belongs to THEFOUNDER-radar.
