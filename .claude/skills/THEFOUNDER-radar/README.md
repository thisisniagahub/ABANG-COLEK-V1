# THEFOUNDER-radar

> Your instrument panel after launch. Decides the few numbers that actually matter for this business (activation, retention, churn, revenue per user, the one funnel step that leaks), sets up how each is measured, and turns this week's numbers into one decision: fix, push or hold. Closes the loop - what the radar sees feeds back into THEFOUNDER-megaphone, THEFOUNDER-magnet and THEFOUNDER-workshop. Use when the user says "what should I measure", "set up my metrics", "why is churn up", "read my numbers", or after the business is live.

## Trigger phrases

- `what should I measure`
- `set up my metrics`
- `why is churn up`
- `read my numbers`

## Apa yang skill ini tidak buat (the fence)

- Never invents, interpolates or rounds up a number it cannot source; an
  unmeasurable metric stays **unmeasured** with a reason.
- Never picks more than five metrics; never replaces a metric mid-week.
- Setting prices belongs to THEFOUNDER-pricetag; building growth loops
  belongs to THEFOUNDER-flywheel - the radar only reads and decides.
