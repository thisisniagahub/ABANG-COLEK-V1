# CHAIN.md — the food chain of THEFOUNDER

The pack organised as one supply chain: value flows from a raw idea to a
growing business, and the last stage feeds the first. Every skill owns one
link; no two skills share a purpose.

## The loop

The chain is a circle, not a line: **radar** (stage 8) feeds what it sees back
into **battlefield/crowd/pulse** (stage 1) and **magnet/megaphone** (stage 2/5),
and **flywheel** (stage 9) feeds **radar** with the one number that proves the
loop turns. A business that only walks the chain once is a business that
walks off a cliff on schedule.

## Gaps this map closed

Before this organisation the pack ended at launch/operate: nothing measured
(the loop could not close), nothing compounded (growth stayed rented), and
nothing protected the cash between launch and profitability. Radar, flywheel,
runway and assembly were added to close exactly those holes.
